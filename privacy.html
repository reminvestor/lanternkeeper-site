Privacy Policy

Lantern Keeper · Effective September 30, 2026

The short version

Lantern Keeper does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress and whether you have unlocked the Full Voyage are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

The Full Voyage in-app purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account information. Apple's handling of purchases is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third parties

Lantern Keeper contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be sent through the Lantern Keeper support page.
