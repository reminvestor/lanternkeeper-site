Lantern Keeper

A quiet puzzle game of light and fog.

Getting help

Found a bug, stuck on a puzzle, or have an idea? Open a support request on the Lantern Keeper support page. We read every one.

Common questions

I'm stuck on a puzzle. Tap the ? button (or the lighthouse) and the next piece to turn will blink. Hold the undo button to restart a puzzle.

What does the Full Voyage include? The first island is free. The one-time Full Voyage purchase unlocks the other four islands (24 puzzles) and the rest of the story. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

I bought the Full Voyage on another device. Open any locked island, tap Unlock the full voyage, then Restore Purchases.

I don't hear any sound. Lantern Keeper follows your iPhone's silent mode. Turn silent mode off to hear the sea and the chimes.
